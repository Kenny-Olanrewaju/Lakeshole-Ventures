Questions

Good to know

Are Miira products medicines?

No. They are wellness supplements and drinks. They are not medicines and are not meant to diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If you are unwell, pregnant, nursing or on medication, speak to your doctor before use.

How do I use them?

Most Miira products come as sachets that you mix with water or hot water as directed on the pack. Always follow the directions printed on your product.

Can I take more than one product?

Miira products are designed to work alongside each other. The manufacturer suggests leaving a gap between different products, so check the pack or ask us for guidance.

How do I pay?

After you send your order on WhatsApp we will confirm the total, payment method and delivery details with you directly.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. Tap the heart on any product card to save it, then use the "Saved" filter to see your picks. Saved items stay on this device.
